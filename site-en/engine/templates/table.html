💡Adding a record

Click the blue "+" button at the top to open the add-record form, or the blue "+" button at the bottom to add a new row right in this table. Fill in the fields and click "Save".

💡Sorting and filtering

Click a column header to sort. Use the search bar above the table or the column filters to find records.

💡Editing and deleting

Click a cell to edit it right in the table. To delete rows, use the button to select records, then remove them with the delete button. Changes apply after confirmation.
